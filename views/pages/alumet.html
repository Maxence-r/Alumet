Aide

Learn how to use your Alumet in every detail.

To create a new column, click "Add a column". If the button is hidden, you may lack admin permissions or need to sign in. After clicking, choose whether participants can post in that column. To edit a column, hover its title and click the edit icon.
To add a post, click the "Add a post" button available in each column if you have administrator permissions. To edit a post, hover over it and click the edit icon.
Some posts may be hidden because of a scheduled publication date or because "Visible only to administrators" is enabled. Sign in with a collaborator account to access every post.
If the edit icon does not appear on hover, you do not own that post. Anonymous posts can only be edited from the computer where they were created. You can always ask an administrator to edit or delete a post for you.
