The end, or maybe not...

Now former students of Clemenceau high school in Nantes, we can say Alumet Education was a huge project for us and a genuine attempt to shake up digital education. Despite Clemenceau's clear support, this was never simple. Because the Nantes academy categorically refused to collaborate and help the tool grow, our chances of success became much smaller. For that reason, we decided not to continue with Alumet version 2.

One thing is certain: Alumet Education taught us a lot. We noticed very strong interest in flashcards. Our mistake may have been trying to build too many tools instead of focusing on the quality and efficiency of one. Even so, flashcards were heavily used, so why stop there...

For now, this is where the Alumet Education V2 adventure ends. Thank you for taking part, and thank you for all the support we received. We hope to see you again soon.
