    Aide

    Learn how to use your Alumet in every detail.

    To add new flashcards, use the "Add flashcards" button. If this button is not visible, you are probably neither a collaborator nor the owner. In that case, ask an administrator to add you. You can also use artificial intelligence to quickly generate content from courses or keywords.
    To review your flashcards, click the "Review" button. You will then choose between two review modes. If you know the answer to a flashcard, drag it to the right; otherwise, drag it to the left. Use the spacebar to reveal the answer.
    Smart review mode is designed to support long-term memory. For that reason, you may need to wait a few days before reviewing your set again. flashcards.
