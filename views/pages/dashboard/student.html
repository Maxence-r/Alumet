To create a new workspace, open the first tab by clicking the small home icon at the top left. Then choose the type of workspace you want and click "New" at the top right. You will then be guided through setup.
To edit workspace settings, open the workspace, then open settings from the right navigation bar. You can then adjust its settings. selon your besoins.
To delete one of your workspaces, open it, then open settings from the right navigation bar and use the delete option.
To share a workspace with another user, open it and click "Share" in the navigation bar. You will then be guided through sharing.
We continuously develop new workspace types for Alumet Education, and they are released gradually. If you do not see one, it is not available to you yet.
For each workspace, click "Help" in the navigation bar. A window will open with frequently asked questions for that workspace. If you do not find your answer, contactez alumet.education@gmail.com.
